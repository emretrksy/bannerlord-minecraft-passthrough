# v0.1.0 — first public release

**English** · [Türkçe](#türkçe)

Minecraft's Steve in Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord battles. Download
**MinecraftPassthrough-v0.1.0.zip** below, run `Install.bat`, start the game from the
**Bannerlord x Minecraft** desktop shortcut, and have a look at
[how to play](https://github.com/emretrksy/bannerlord-minecraft-passthrough#how-to-play).

**Requires:** Bannerlord v1.4.8 on Steam (windowed or borderless) · Minecraft: Java Edition with the
official Minecraft Launcher (the installer sets up Fabric and a profile for 26.3) · Windows 10/11.

### What's in it
- Steve plays in every battle with Minecraft's movement, items, inventory and combat.
- A shared world: Bannerlord's ground exists in Minecraft, Steve can build with blocks,
  and TNT, creepers, fire charges, snowballs and eggs all work on soldiers.
- Horseback fighting, soldiers flashing red when hit, a silent stand-in for your character.
- Campaign: Steve's own inventory in your save, loot from soldiers, a Minecraft trader and smith in
  every town, skill experience, wounds carried between Steve and your hero, the bag lost when
  you're taken prisoner.
- Custom battles start with the netherite kit. The F10 panel pauses the battle and gives you five
  kits, any item (with search), revive and invulnerability.
- Pausing Bannerlord (the Esc menu or any other pause) pauses Minecraft too.
- English and Turkish.
- One-click setup: `Install.bat` installs everything, including a Minecraft Launcher profile with
  settings that keep Minecraft from crashing on some PCs, and a desktop shortcut that starts both
  games.

Questions and bug reports: [Psicodart Games Discord](https://discord.gg/R4qStgw9bk)

### Known limitations
- Frames are shared up to 1920 × 1200.
- Bannerlord's lighting doesn't reach Steve.
- Single player only; don't use the ReShade add-on build in online games with anti-cheat.

### Neler var
- Steve her savaşta oynuyor; hareket, eşyalar, envanter ve dövüş Minecraft'taki gibi.
- Ortak dünya: Bannerlord'un zemini Minecraft'ta var, Steve blokla inşa edebiliyor; TNT,
  creeper, ateş topu, kartopu ve yumurta askerlere işliyor.
- At üstünde dövüş, vurulan askerlerin kırmızı yanıp sönmesi, karakterinin yerine sessiz bir vekil.
- Kampanya: kayıt dosyasında Steve'in kendi envanteri, askerlerden ganimet, her şehirde Minecraft
  tüccarı ve demircisi, yetenek deneyimi, Steve ile kahraman arasında taşınan yaralar, esir
  düşünce kaybedilen çanta.
- Özel savaşlar netherite kitle başlıyor. F10 paneli savaşı durduruyor; beş kit, arama kutusuyla her
  eşya, dirilme ve ölümsüzlük veriyor.
- Bannerlord durunca Minecraft da duruyor (Esc menüsü ya da başka bir duraklatma).
- İngilizce ve Türkçe.
- Tek tık kurulum: `Install.bat` her şeyi kuruyor; bazı bilgisayarlarda Minecraft'ın çökmesini
  önleyen ayarlarla bir Minecraft Launcher profili ve iki oyunu birlikte açan bir masaüstü kısayolu
  dahil.

Sorular ve hata bildirimleri: [Psicodart Games Discord](https://discord.gg/R4qStgw9bk)
